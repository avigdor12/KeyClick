The Login Process, Registration and Source-Computer Protection
Registered Customer & Source-Computer Security
New Customer & Installing the Application
Quick Login: Entering first and last name, email address and password.
Compatibility Check: If there's an error, a message is displayed: "Customer not found".
Source-Computer Protection, Privacy: One customer per instance, and only on the computer where they first registered
Blocking Remote Access: Logging in from a different computer isn't possible, and an appropriate message is shown to the customer.
A Short Registration Process: Choose Login -> Register -> Choose a personalized plan.
Launch and Trial Period: Use of the system is free of charge. A notice will be sent at the end of the period.
Download and Installation: A built-in process for downloading and installing the home budget management app.
Simple Instructions: Choose a download location, run the file and follow the installation instructions.
